# The Mossbound Atlas Wiki

Find a path. Make a home. Bring the world together.

An offline, single-player pixel art wilderness RPG. The world is seeded and persistent: your camps, gardens, restored waystones, discoveries and lost belongings remain part of the same journey.

This reference describes the implemented game. Current content includes **143 items, 115 recipes, 41 building pieces, five regions, 30 discovery sites, 25 creatures, eight travelers and 16 rotating requests**. Repeatable trials open deeper challenges after each completion.
