# Combat and field guide

Aim with the mouse or right stick. Blade and axe favor close combat; spears reach farther; bows and resonance focuses fire projectiles. Attack windups, hits and recovery have distinct animation phases. Dodge before an attack lands, then use the recovery window. Sprinting and actions consume stamina; short recovery delays prevent instant refills.

Coats, charms, skills and food effects change your preparation. The field guide records observed creatures, kills, behaviors and supplies. These are base Normal statistics; difficulty changes hostile health and incoming damage.
